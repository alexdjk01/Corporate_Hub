Adapting to Changing Circumstances
During an university project, I worked with 2 of my colleagues to develop a web application for student grading.
In the last 2 weeks before submission, we found out that the logic was wrong and needed to be redone.
In order so solve this optimally I:
-reassessed the original approach
-keep in touch with my team and point out mistakes
-communicate the changes made 
-created a timetable so we knew when other are available to work on the project
